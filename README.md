# Blocked – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 2026**

Blocked respects your privacy. This game does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

---

## **Information We Collect**
Blocked does **not** collect any personal data, including but not limited to:

- Name, email address, phone number  
- Device ID  
- Location data  
- Photos, media, or files  
- Contacts  
- Microphone or camera data  
- Advertising identifiers  

No personal data is gathered, stored, or transmitted.

---

## **Crash Logs and Diagnostic Data**
The game may use Google Play's built-in crash reporting system.

These reports are:
- Anonymous  
- Contain no personal or identifying information  
- Used only to improve game stability  

Blocked does not store or manage any crash logs manually.

---

## **In-App Purchases**
Blocked does **not** include any in-app purchases or payment systems.

No financial information is collected.

---

## **Third-Party Services**
Blocked does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

---

## **Children's Privacy**
Blocked is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from children or any other users.
