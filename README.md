# Blocked – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 25 July 2026**

This privacy policy explains what data the mobile game **Blocked** (package name
`com.arargames.blocked`, "the Game") processes, why, and what choices you have.

The Game is developed and published by **Arar Games** ("we", "us").
Contact: **arargame@hotmail.com**

---

## Summary

The Game itself does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number,
photos, contacts, microphone or camera access, and it does not track your precise
location.

However, the Game **does** show advertisements, **does** offer in-app purchases,
and **does** integrate Google services. These involve data processing that is
described in detail below.

---

## Data We Process

### 1. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through
**Google AdMob**.

To do this, Google may collect and process:

- Your **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID). The Game declares the
  `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission for this purpose.
- Device information such as device model, operating system version, language and
  screen characteristics.
- **Approximate location derived from your IP address** (country/region level, not
  precise GPS location).
- Interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks.

This data is used to deliver, measure and — where permitted — personalise ads, and
to detect invalid traffic and fraud.

Google acts as an independent controller and/or processor for this data. Its
handling is governed by Google's own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### 2. Consent Management (Google User Messaging Platform)

If you are located in the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
Switzerland**, the Game shows a consent form the first time you play, provided by
the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)**.

Your consent choices are stored **locally on your device** (including IAB TCF
standard values). Personalised advertising is only used if you consent to it.

**You can change or fully withdraw your consent at any time** from within the
Game: open **Options → PRIVACY OPTIONS**. This reopens the same consent form.
Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

### 3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game offers optional in-app purchases through **Google Play Billing**:

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `10_gold_product` | 10 gold |
| `50_gold_product` | 50 gold |
| `100_gold_product` | 100 gold |
| `REMOVE_ADS` | Removes advertisements |

All payments are processed by **Google Play**. We never see or store your card
number, bank details or billing address.

The Game stores **purchase tokens** returned by Google Play in its local save file.
This is done solely to prevent the same purchase from being granted twice. These
tokens stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.

### 4. Google Play Games Services

The Game integrates **Google Play Games Services** for sign-in and leaderboards.
If you sign in, your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and player ID)
and your submitted scores are processed by Google and may be visible on public
leaderboards.

Signing in is optional. The Game is fully playable without it.

### 5. Local Game Data

Your progress is stored in a save file on your own device (`savegame.json`). It
contains, among other things: level progress, scores, gold balance, unlocked
skills and your Game settings.

It also contains a **randomly generated player identifier (GUID)** created on your
device. This identifier is not linked to your real identity and is not sent to us
or to any third party.

This file is not uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the Game removes it.

### 6. Notifications

The Game may schedule **local reminder notifications** (for example, about your
daily reward streak). These are generated entirely on your device. No data leaves
your device for this purpose, and no push notification service is used.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

### 7. Crash and Performance Data

Google Play may automatically collect anonymous crash and performance reports.
We use these only to fix bugs and improve stability.

## Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland:

- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalised advertising and any associated
  storage of, or access to, information on your device. Requested through the
  consent form and revocable at any time.
- **Contractual necessity** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for processing in-app purchases
  you have chosen to make.
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — for fraud and invalid-traffic
  prevention, security, and basic non-personalised advertising that keeps the Game
  free.

## Data Retention

- Consent choices: stored on your device until you change them, clear the app's
  data, or uninstall the Game.
- Save data and purchase tokens: stored on your device until you reset your
  progress in the Game or uninstall it.
- Data held by Google: retained according to Google's own retention policies.

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, rectify, erase and
port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any
time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection
authority.

Because we do not collect or hold identifiable personal data ourselves, most such
requests need to be directed to **Google**, which processes the advertising,
payment and Play Games data described above. You can manage a large part of this
yourself:

- Reset or delete your advertising ID: **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**
- Change your consent: **Options → PRIVACY OPTIONS** inside the Game
- Manage your Google data: [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy)

For anything else, contact us at **arargame@hotmail.com**.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is **not directed at children under 13** (or the equivalent minimum age in

programme.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided
data through the Game, contact us at **arargame@hotmail.com** and we will take
